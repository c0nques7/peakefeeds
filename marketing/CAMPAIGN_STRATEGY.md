# Peake Feeds: Zero-Cost Grassroots Marketing Campaign

## 🎯 Objective
Share the genuine process of building Peake Feeds. Avoid "hype" or "influencer" language. Focus on personal frustrations with current social media, interesting technical challenges solved, and the honest journey of a developer building a tool they actually want to use.

## 🗓️ Schedule (3x / Week)
Consistency is key. We will post on **Monday**, **Wednesday**, and **Friday**.

### **Monday: The Problem (My Perspective)**
*   **Theme:** Personal observations on trust, bots, and online noise.
*   **Goal:** Share *why* this matters to me personally, not just "society".
*   **Format:** Conversational thoughts, "I was thinking about...", genuine questions to the reader.

### **Wednesday: The Solution (Dev Log / How it Works)**
*   **Theme:** The Truth Layer, Ad-Sponsored Gas, specific features.
*   **Goal:** Geek out a bit. Explain the "cool part" of the code.
*   **Format:** "Here's how I fixed X", "I finally got Y working", simple explanations of complex tech.

### **Friday: Community & Vision (The Journey)**
*   **Theme:** Open Source, Progress updates, Real talk.
*   **Goal:** Honest status update. What worked, what didn't.
*   **Format:** "This week was hard/great...", sharing a specific win or loss.

## 🚀 Key Talking Points
1.  **"I'm building this because I want to use it."**
2.  **"Web3 tech should be invisible, just useful."**
3.  **"Here is the code, verify it yourself."**

## 🛠️ Zero-Cost Tactics
*   **Be a Human:** Reply to people like a person, not a brand account.
*   **Share the Struggle:** Bugs are content. Fixing them is a story.
*   **Open Source:** Point to the repo often.
