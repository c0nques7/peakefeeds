## 🔵 Wednesday: The Solution (Dev Log / Tech)

**Post 1:**
> One of the biggest hurdles with Web3 apps is gas fees. I don't want people to have to buy ETH just to post.
>
> So I implemented this "Ad-Mediator" thing. Basically, you watch a 15s ad, and the protocol pays your gas.
>
> It was a pain to wire up, but seeing it work for the first time was pretty satisfying.

**Post 2:**
> Finally got the signing logic working on the backend.
>
> 1. You write a post.
> 2. Your wallet signs it.
> 3. It gets anchored on Optimism.
>
> It’s nice to know that once it's posted, nobody (including me) can stealth-edit it later.

**Post 3:**
> "Truth Layer" sounds fancy, but it's really just a simple concept:
>
> Cryptographic signatures for everything.
>
> I'm trying to strip away all the complexity so you don't even notice it's happening in the background. That's the goal, anyway.

**Post 4:**
> I've been thinking a lot about privacy vs. verification.
>
> You should be able to prove you're human without giving away your passport. That's why I'm using wallet signatures.
>
> It's not perfect yet, but it feels like the right direction.

**Post 5:**
> Spent the last few days cleaning up the mobile navigation. 📱
>
> Web3 apps usually feel clunky on phones. I'm trying really hard to make this feel like a native app.
>
> Sidebar is looking much cleaner now. Small wins!
